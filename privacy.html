Privacy Policy

Discord Card Bot

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Information We Store

The bot may store Discord user IDs, virtual currency balances, card inventories and the Discord channel selected for card spawning.

2. Messages

Commands sent to the bot may be processed to provide requested features. Ordinary messages are not intentionally stored permanently unless required by a specific feature.

3. AI Feature

If the AI command is enabled, text submitted to that command may be sent to an AI service to generate a response. Do not submit passwords, payment information or other sensitive information.

4. How Data Is Used

Stored data is used to operate features such as cards, inventory, virtual currency, trading and leaderboards.

5. Data Sharing

Data may be processed by third-party services required for specific bot features. The bot owner does not sell user data.

6. Data Retention

Game data may remain stored while the bot is operating. Data may be deleted or reset when technically possible.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect stored data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot owner through the Discord server where the bot is operated.
